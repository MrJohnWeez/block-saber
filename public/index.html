Block Saber

Turn Beat Saber map zips into Minecraft datapacks for the Block Saber game world.

Drag one or more Beat Saber map .zip files here to convert them to Minecraft datapacks.

    Installing your converted packs

    1. Download each song's datapack with the button on its row above, and drop the zips (unzipped) into your Block Saber world's datapacks folder: .minecraft/saves/<world>/datapacks/.
    2. Download the songs pack ("Block Saber Songs") and put that single zip into .minecraft/resourcepacks/ — do not unzip it.
    3. In Minecraft, open Options → Video Settings → Resource Packs, move "Block Saber Songs" to the selected list, and apply. Re-download and replace it whenever you convert new songs.
    4. Open your Block Saber world and run /reload (or re-enter the world) to load the new songs.
    Minecraft 26.3 or newer is required. Song packs made by this site use the new song contract and are not compatible with packs from the old 1.18.2-era generator — delete any old Block Saber song datapacks and resource packs before installing. The base game will refuse mismatched packs in chat.
